## Missing Characters

### Female Hunter (Clan Hoskuld) — NEVER IMPLEMENTED

The Character List describes her as:
- `"Comes from generations of hunters"`
- `"Wishes for bigger game — harpies would be good challenge"`
- `"Loves husband dearly but can't understand why he is happy mucking about in the mud"`

Writer assignment: Brian M. → Sheri (handwritten reassignment).
Red X on schedule confirms this was never completed.

**Ragnar is NOT the Female Hunter.** Ragnar is male, was added later (not in
Character List 1.0), and has his own schedule entry on the resource schedule.
He fills a similar hunting role for Clan Hoskuld but is a separate character.

### Male Farmer (Clan Hoskuld) — STATUS UNCERTAIN

The Character List describes him as:
- `"Trying to instill in his son a love of the land"`
- `"Proud of wife for how she keeps meat on the table"`
- `"Proud of how he can bring life from such poor soil"`

Writer assignment: Brian M. → Sheri.
No usecode file matches this character. Either:
1. He was cut before implementation began
2. His dialogue was never written (like the Hunter)
3. He was merged into another NPC during development

His wife (the Hunter) was also never implemented, so likely neither was he.

### Female Farmer (Clan Gunnar) — STATUS UNCERTAIN

Character List describes her as:
- `"We've always done it this way."`
- Conservative, devout, opposes the Inventor
- `"Wishes they would throw the inventor out with Ezrekal"`

Writer assignment: SHERI.
No usecode file matches. Hrodny's random barks (`"Fools, they are! Help them, I could!"`)
suggest the Inventor-vs-Conservative tension exists in the world but the Female
Farmer's side of it was never scripted.

### Female Child of Elder (Gunnar) — CUT

Struck through in Character List. `"Mother died climbing the cliffs. Never far from daddy."`
This is consistent with Atli's "lost wife" backstory but was explicitly removed
from the game design.

## Implications for Dialogue Writing

1. **The Hunter NPC** (Task B2) should be written as the Female Hunter from
   Character List, married to the Male Farmer, in Clan Hoskuld territory.
   Ragnar is a separate NPC who can interact with her.

2. **The Male Farmer** could appear in Hunter dialogue as a referenced character
   even if he has no separate dialogue file.

3. **The Female Farmer (Gunnar)** could appear in Hrodny or Ingyald dialogue as
   a referenced character representing the conservative opposition.

4. **All Zealan NPCs speak in "Elean Speak"** (inverted syntax) — see
   `speech-patterns.md` for the style guide.
