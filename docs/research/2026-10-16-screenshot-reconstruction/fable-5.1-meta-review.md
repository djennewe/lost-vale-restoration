# Meta-review of AstraReview — FableMeta2 (2026-10-16)

Scope: re-derived every Astra number I could from the repo files (vignette JSONs, `shared/data/lv-1994-art/`, the 19 GIFs, `vignette_paint.py`/`item_sorter.py`, built `U8SHAPES.FLX`/`TYPEFLAG.DAT`) with my own replay in the eval kernel. Read-only; scratch in `/tmp/lv-shots/review/FableMeta/` (`measurements.json`, crop images). All 19 vignettes were replayed from JSON (Astra replayed 4). Guesses are marked [INFERENCE].

## Summary verdict

Astra's review is **substantially correct and its numbers reproduce exactly** (every figure I re-measured — 99.921/99.941, 82.489/82.823, 99.902/99.914, 95.730/96.037, 98.446, 233 classes / diameter 40, the GIF sizes, the built-shape dimensions, the 42 Felicitar pixels, the 118-px screen02 panel discrepancy — came out identical). Its ranking is miscalibrated in two directions:

1. **The circularity critique (M2) is overweighted.** For the stated goal ("recover the 1994 art, then rebuild around it"), extracting a frame from screen11 and pasting it back is not a flaw; the question is whether the extracted frames are *real reusable art*, and the independent check is cross-shot consistency. I ran it: cloud f0/f1/f2 cut from screen11 reproduce independent tiles in screen13 (f0 1019/1024, f2 1012/1024), screen12 (f1 914/1024, f2 936/1024) and screen01 (f2 1006/1024, f0 632/632 on a half-visible tile), and **every mismatch is a contiguous occluder blob** (a column + its cap for f1 in screen12 — components of 84 and 26 px; the cliff at the left diamond tip for f2 in screen01; a neighbouring tile's tip in screen13). The banded column is found nine times at 698/698 in screen12 after being cut from screen11's obelisk drums. So 80.5 of screen11's 99.94 points are cross-validated art, not in-sample fit. What *is* in-sample: the plain column (10.3 pts, screen11 only), the sun shield half (0.6), f3/f4 (1.3). The fix is provenance labelling, not a method change.

2. **Two things Astra missed are bigger than several of its Majors:** (a) the three cave shots' headline numbers (05: 95.5%, 07: 95.2%, 10: 91.9%) count unpainted void as black-and-matched for **12.2 / 8.3 / 4.9 points** of the frame — Astra measured black-target fractions only on the four non-cave shots (1-2%) and concluded black "is not the cause"; (b) **screen22.json does not reproduce its published render** (854 px differ; the published render has 747 shot-matching pixels the JSON lacks; JSON replays at 95.43%, published 96.61%). Also the vignettes' Avatar is at z=2 on a z=1 flat in every Cloud shot; in-engine it will settle to z=1, so the in-engine fixture is guaranteed a 1-px Avatar offset unless the cloud z/hotspot scheme is changed.

Net: **B1 is the only true blocker and only for its listed assets** (fire pillar, screen19 elder, f3/f4 as "complete"). Everything else is doc wording, a replay-manifest cleanup, and decisions to label. The reconstruction is good evidence; the main document over-claims it.

## Which pin moves are pixel-forced vs design (M4 made concrete)

Forced by the pixels (relative to the chosen window): god spacing 384×512 on retail plinths 37:10/13 with gods at plinth-top z (given plinth identification); altar on the floor with the shield on it (no dais); nothing but floor inside the screen04 frame (so wall, skull 331, dais, candelabra are *out of this window*); shield halves beside the obelisk / in the nook; skeletons beside the obelisk; the temple stair as 8-unit cobble steps with a shelf ≥ one stair-height above the pit floor; shore at z 8 wherever the screen02 window lands; the Map 10 tiers (112/104/64) *relative to each window's lowest ground*; 866 out of the screen07 window; the summit tiers 8/64/120/176 relative to screen01's base.

## What Astra missed

1. **Cave-void points.** screen05/07/10 leave 12.85/8.84/4.95% of the frame unpainted; 7259/4921/2871 of those pixels are shot-black and are counted as matches. All-frame-painted scores: **83.3 / 86.6 / 87.0%** (lenient). Defensible — the engine clears to black — but the doc must say "void counted as black" and the convention must be uniform (Vale/Village count unpainted as *mismatch*, 0.2-2.4%). Astra's "black is not the cause" was measured on the wrong four shots.
2. **screen22.json ≠ published render** (854 px; 747 published-only matches in the top-left dirt, x0-187/y0-79; `screen22_saved_vs_replay_x3.png`). The JSON is the deliverable; it scores 95.43, not 96.61. [INFERENCE: a final frame-swap was rendered but not written back.] screen19/20 reproduce exactly.
3. **Actor settling on the z=1 cloud.** Cloud vignettes put every object and the Avatar at z=2 on a zd=0 flat at z=1. In-engine the Avatar (and any NPC) stands on the support surface, z=1 [INFERENCE: gravity/`CurrentMap` support], so the fixture capture is off by one row on the Avatar — and `/playtest` will report it as a mismatch. Either accept the engine-standard encoding (cloud z=0 with yoff 32, objects on 31-residues; costs 0.35 pts on screen11 from the shield-half parity) or document the 1-px delta.
4. **The shot camera is not Avatar-centred.** Avatar screen hotspots range from (36,125) (screen11) to (214,170) (screen04) to (78,115) (screen09); a following camera at screen centre cannot produce these under any crop ≤ 49 px. So the 1994 shots were taken with a free/scripted camera, and the fixture must script the camera at `(camera.sx0, sy0)` world-equivalent and place the Avatar at the vignette's actor position with the exact frame. A player standing in these spots will never see these frames; "exact" applies to the fixture, not to gameplay. (Also the 19 crops are different sizes, so each needs its own crop rectangle.)
5. **`test_vignettes.py` as planned cannot run on the current `Painter`:** `vignette_paint.py:34-35` hard-wires `TYPEFLAG.DAT.bak`/`U8SHAPES.FLX.bak`; the test needs the *built* artifacts with the final IDs, and actors are not in FIXED.DAT so the test must inject them from the JSON.
6. **Doc arithmetic:** "16 of the 19 score 92% or higher" — the table has 15 (screen10 is 91.9% in the same table).
7. **screen01 note vs data:** the art note says "11 tiles registered exactly"; the JSON has 9 `CLOUD1994` items, and only two of them are ≥97% unoccluded in the shot (the others are 25-77% because of occluders). Fine as evidence, wrong as a count.
8. **Palette-index 255 is a real teal** ((24,125,125)), the extraction encodes transparency as 255. No shot pixel in 02/09 maps to 255 (checked), so no Water Zombie pixel was lost — but the convention is a latent trap for any future teal sprite; `png2shape.py` must be checked for it before the art step.
9. **Art directory hygiene:** 18 of 65 PNGs under `shared/data/lv-1994-art/` are RGB/RGBA previews and comparison sheets mixed with the P-mode assets; a builder that globs the directory will break.
10. **screen10 strict vs lenient gap is 11 points** (75.9 vs 87.0 all-frame-painted) — the one shot where the palette-class tolerance is doing real work (blood/dais reds, probably [INFERENCE]). Worth stating when "exact" is promised.
