## What moved, and why

The three shots the static slice rebuilt (07, 12, 13) reproduce its
in-engine numbers to the hundredth (89.90 / 96.37 / 93.99 in its report).

Every other change is a transient, not map content. For each shot the new
640×400 capture was diffed against the 10-17 capture taken from the same
teleport spot (`docs/playtest-2026-10-25-phaseD-verify/V1-*-old-vs-new.png`):

- **screen17 (−1.41), screen22 (−2.56), screen16 (−0.90).** The differing
  pixels are retail GHOST 411 at a different point on its path, and the
  Avatar's pose.
- **screen19 (−3.43).** The differing pixels are the ghost and Atli (now
  888, the 1994 elder) walking his loiter outside the doorway. In the 10-17
  capture he was inside the hut. The pass-to-pass range of 0.98 points is his
  position.
- **screen09 (+7.16).** The differing pixels are the waterfall's animation
  phase.
- **screen05, 15 and 21 (up).** The differing pixels are the fire/lava
  animation, the ghost and the Avatar.
- **screen02 (0.00).** The capture is pixel-identical to 10-17's.

The phase-D actors (the Water Zombie 872, Atli 888, the children 889/890) do
not raise screen02/09/19/20 in-engine. The zombies exist only after a pier
trigger hatches them, and the NPCs walk their schedules, so none of them
