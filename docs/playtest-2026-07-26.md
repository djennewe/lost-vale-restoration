## P0 — do these first

### 1. The conversation-freeze theory (never observed, huge blast radius)

**Why first:** it is inferred from source, not seen. Our `enterConvo()` calls
intrinsic `0x9A`; ScummVM leaves that index unimplemented and returns 0; and
`kernel.cpp:311` treats objid 0 as a **wildcard matching every object**. If
that chain holds, every conversation freezes every NPC on the map, across all
19 conversation files. It costs nothing to check and it invalidates a lot of
other observations if true.

```
Cheat::toggle
MainActor::teleport 10 7680 6400 0
```

Talk to anyone. While the conversation is open, **watch the terminal** for:

```
LV: I_resetRef item=0
```

Then close the conversation and walk around.

- **Expected if the theory is wrong:** no such line, and NPCs who were walking
  before the conversation are still walking after it.
- **Expected if the theory is right:** the line appears, and every NPC on the
  map is frozen in place afterwards, permanently.

Record which. If it is right, stop and report before judging anything else,
because frozen NPCs will look like a dozen other bugs.

- **Expected:** plays once, latch set, no repeat.
- **Known and expected:** his *older* poison thread stays invisible, because it
  is gated on `avatarPoisoned`, which nothing writes while the poison is off.

## P2 — diagnostic, no fix expected yet

### 8. Three more eggs with the wrong quality

Found while writing this. A shape-73 egg names the class it runs by
`quality + 0x47F`, and three placements carry small numbers that were meant as
labels. **The base game places no shape-73 eggs at all, so none of these is a
copied idiom.** All three currently run an unrelated base-game class.

| Where | Teleport | Comment in the builder says | Actually runs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Map 10 | `MainActor::teleport 10 20480 17152 0` | "SHADES trigger" | MONEGG (1153) |
| Map 18 | `MainActor::teleport 18 3072 5760 0` | "triggers POTION usecode" | EXCUTION (1155) |
| Map 18 | `MainActor::teleport 18 3072 8320 8` | "FREE ceremony trigger" | STRATHAT (1156) |

Walk over each and record what happens. EXCUTION is 6 KB of base-game scene
code, so this one may be visibly dramatic.

**The Map 10 one is the important one.** It is the ceremony that contains the
**only line in the entire corpus that cures the poison**. It was placed all
along; it just needs quality **294** instead of 2. That single number is the
difference between the cure being reachable and not, and it changes the
decision-A picture: the ceremony is not unwired, it is misnumbered.

Do not change it before deciding. Correcting it runs a 10.5 KB scene that has
never executed, which is precisely why decision A chose to disable the poison
rather than enable the cure blind.
