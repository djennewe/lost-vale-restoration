Walk

WASD or arrows walk (screen directions, as the game's mouse does), Shift runs, Q/E step to the surface above/below, C centres. Drag to pan, wheel to zoom. No collision: you walk through walls. F leaves a note where the Avatar stands; right-click leaves one on the spot under the cursor.
