## Blog Post: "Looking Back on 20 years — The Lost Vale"

Sheri Graner Ray describes working on the Lost Vale expansion after an exhausting
10-month crunch period on Ultima 8 base game. She and her team (Lisa Smith and Rob Corell)
were called into Warren Spector's office to join the project, though they were initially
unenthusiastic about continuing with Ultima VIII.

### Development Details

- The Lost Vale featured two isolated tribes in a mountain valley who had abandoned their
  old gods
- A central feature was "dialogue written in Yoda-speak" — this "seemed like SUCH a funny,
  good idea" initially but became tedious after months of writing. She eventually had to
  complete the translation herself
- This is the "Elean Speak" referenced in the design documents (see: "Elean Speak Exerpt.pdf")

### Notable Design Elements

- A **PTSD-affected veteran character** requiring player assistance
- A **"wise woman" character** who poisons the player to ensure quest completion
  (this is Ezrekal — she poisons the Avatar to force them to complete the god-freeing quest)
- An abandoned **Cloud City** where equipment gets magnetically sealed to an obelisk
  (the MAGNET.UNK usecode file)
- A **"TinyTar" shrinking spell** that started as a developer joke but became plot-critical

### Self-Critique

Ray candidly describes the candlestick puzzle mechanism as "probably the LAMEST design
I've EVER done!" (the CANTRK.UNK usecode file)

### Cancellation

The team completed and gold-mastered Lost Vale, but the night before submission to
publishers, Warren Spector informed them the project was cancelled due to poor Ultima VIII
sales. The team was left without assigned projects.

## GameBanshee Interview (March 2013)

### On Cancellation Despite Going Gold

> It was directly due to a lack of sales. Back then you couldn't do anything as DLC — it
> had to have a box and go on a shelf with commensurate marketing dollars added to it. It
> came down to straight numbers. If the same percentage of U8 players bought the add-on as
> U7 and U7.5 players bought those add-ons, it simply didn't justify the cost of
> manufacturing and distributing the U8 add-on.

### Industry Context at Cancellation

At the time of cancellation:
- Ultima 9 was beginning development
- Discussions for Ultima Online were underway
- Crusader was in full production
- Wing Commander 3 was being developed
- EA was "starting to make noise about a cut back in headcount for ORIGIN"
- Company attention had moved on from Ultima 8, seen as "a less than successful chapter
  in Ultima history"
- There was "no enthusiasm for Lost Vale"

### Development Timeline

- Approximately **three months** to complete (contradicts some sources saying four;
  may exclude pre-production in April)
- Team was exhausted after U8's **nine-month** development with **12–18 hour workdays**
- During the final month, developers received Sundays off

### Design Philosophy

- Designed to "answer questions" about the game's story
- Did NOT address fan criticisms of U8's action-heavy approach
- Maintained U8's action focus

### On the Tiny-tar Spell

> "Really funny" and "a huge deal" for the team, though acknowledging it seems minor by
> modern standards.

### Internal Reception

> The expansion pack fit within the theme of U8, added to the story, answered some
> questions, and was met with approval from the QA team.

### On Archival

> No gold copy exists today, as ORIGIN wasn't archiving projects during the EA merger
> period.
