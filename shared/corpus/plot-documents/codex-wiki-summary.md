# Ultima VIII: The Lost Vale — Ultima Codex Wiki Summary

Source: https://wiki.ultimacodex.com/wiki/Ultima_VIII:_The_Lost_Vale

## Overview

Ultima VIII: The Lost Vale is a cancelled add-on for Ultima VIII: Pagan. The expansion
"was ready to be duplicated for release before Electronic Arts decided against shipping it."

## Story

The Lost Vale served as the original homeland of the Zealans. During conflicts between the
Zealans and Pagans, the Elemental Titans attacked the Zealan gods during a Great Council,
trapping three deities — **Felicitar**, **Doloras**, and **Timyra** — in a cloud city atop
a mountain. The Titans destroyed the **Shield of the Ancients** (used for divine
communication) and sealed the entire valley through the combined efforts of **Lithos** and
**Hydros**, creating a hidden dungeon with magical seals.

The Zealans interpreted this as divine abandonment. High Priestess **Ezrakel** failed to
rejoin the broken Shield. Two young boys secretly stole the Shield pieces to reach the cloud
city for help but died attempting the journey. Their families blamed each other, splitting
Zealan civilization into two weakened communities that drifted further apart.

The **Avatar** receives a magical message from **Lord British** (sent via **Nystul**)
revealing people on Pagan who could help defeat the Titans. **Mythran** directs the Avatar
to find a dungeon entrance behind double doors on the Plateau. Explorer **Galjeron**'s ghost
provides access information. The dungeon contains four elemental sections, with the Lost Vale
entrance blocked by pillars representing Earth, Air, Fire, and Water. Magical gems from each
section destroy corresponding pillars.

The Avatar's mission involves freeing the three trapped gods, healing the Zealan schism,
and repairing the Shield of Ancients. Ezrakel would join the Avatar to ascend the cloud city
and release the imprisoned deities.

## Production

Development occurred over "four months, between April and August of 1994." Designer team
leads were **Brian Martin** and **Sheri Graner Ray**, with approximately seven team members
total.

Despite completion, poor sales of Ultima VIII and previous expansions (Forge of Virtue
and The Silver Seed) caused executives to cancel distribution due to insufficient profit
projections. The project remained unarchived; data was lost over time. Only screenshots,
plot documents, and box artwork survived verification as authentic.

"In 2025, part of the source code (sadly only a portion of it) was discovered and put online."

## Trivia

- A **Bag of Reagents** item would eliminate the need to collect reagents individually;
  Ezrakel would reward this after freeing the Ancient Ones
- The freed gods would gift a symbolic rejoined shield maximizing armor value
- A new spell called **Tiny-tar** enabled puzzle-solving in the cloud city; source code
  remnants indicated it functioned as a potion
