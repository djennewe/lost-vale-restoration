# Ultima 8 Omissions — Dino's Guide

Source: https://gigi.nullneuron.net/ultima/u8/omission.php
Also: https://gigi.nullneuron.net/ultima/u8/u8tlv.php

## Cut Content from Base Game

The guide documents several features omitted from Ultima 8, attributed to EA's rushed
release. References to missing content appear throughout the game's usecode files.

### Programmer's Playland
Map index 1, confirmed accessible via Pentagram in October 2020. Contains "an assortment
of NPCs" arranged to recreate familiar game scenes like the docks execution and skeleton
hallways. Inaccessible in the shipping game.

### Brimstone Cliffs
Map index 13, listed in Divination focus data between Daemon's Crag and Obsidian Fortress.
The map exists but "is completely empty" with no implemented content.

### Treasure Cove
Listed on the world map and in Divination descriptions. Evidence suggests "the Slayer
Quest _is_ Treasure Cove" — the quest served as a placeholder for this intended location.

### Vengeance Bay
Mentioned in the game manual as a northern location where "the Zealan leader's ship" was
pulled underwater by Hydros. The broken ship appears on the world map; connection to
Carthax Lake was apparently severed.

### The Key of the Necromancer
A third necromantic key exists in game data with usecode references, suggesting players
may have become "the Necromancer" and inherited it.

### Ghoul Invasion
Usecode dialogue references an undead attack on Tenebrae that never materialized in the
final plot.

## The Lost Vale (from Dino's dedicated page)

An unproduced add-on featuring "additional plot, additional landscape and tilesets, the
other three gods and many other things."

### Intended Features
- Accessed through locked doors at Bonecrusher's cave on the Plateau
- Hidden valley created by the Titans of Earth and Water
- The City of the Ancient Gods suspended in clouds
- New monsters, magic items, and increased character stats
- A shield with symbols of the three Zealan gods, broken in two
- The "tiny-tar" spell allowing players to shrink themselves
- Several additional hours of gameplay

### Plot Details (from designer Sheri Graner Ray)
Story involved "a group of people shut off from the main population" who experienced
tragedy when two young boys died, causing a schism among survivors. Players would awaken
the Ancient Gods and "heal the schism," discovering the gods were actually imprisoned in
the cloud city rather than asleep.

### Development History
- Originally planned for fall 1994 release
- Expected retail price: $25–$35
- Designed by Sheri Graner Ray
- "Tiny-tar" spell developed by John Watson
- Plot documents released in March 2014 (via Origin Gallery)
- Work-in-progress usecode discovered in February 2025

### Physical Evidence
A one-of-a-kind retail box was discovered in 2005, authenticated by artist Denis Loubet,
and sold at auction for $1,923.

## Relevance to Restoration

The omissions page confirms several things relevant to our restoration:
1. The double doors on the Plateau are the canonical entrance point
2. Bonecrusher's cave is the specific location
3. The base game already has hooks for LV content (Zealan references, map stubs)
4. Cut base-game content (Brimstone Cliffs, Vengeance Bay) may need consideration
5. The Ghoul Invasion and Necromancer Key suggest unfinished base-game plot threads
   that may or may not connect to LV
